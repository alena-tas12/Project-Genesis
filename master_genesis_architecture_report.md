## 1. Project Overview & Philosophical Origin

### 1.1 The Chrono Principle & Closed Architecture Design
One of the key lessons learned from building complex AGI-like architectures (such as Chrono) is that every time one problem is solved, another subsystem emerges. This creates an open-ended loop where software projects expand indefinitely without reaching a state of completion.

**Project Genesis** intentionally breaks this cycle by defining a **closed, finite architecture with fixed boundaries**. 

- **Finite Scope**: This project is an *Educational Systems Simulation & Research Platform*, NOT an evolving personal assistant, administrative LMS, or student chatbot.
- **Finish Line (v1.0 Definition)**: The architecture is complete when every module has a fixed interface and defined responsibility. Future educational theories are added strictly as **configurations, rule sets, and parameters**, rather than by altering the simulator's underlying codebase.

### 1.2 System Identity & Specifications
- **Project Name**: Project Genesis
- **Version**: 1.0 Research Architecture
- **Type**: Educational Systems Simulation & Research Platform
- **Scope**: Finite (Closed Architecture)
- **Primary Goal**: Objective comparison of educational architectures via multi-agent population simulation.

### 1.3 The Karunya Educational Philosophy: Externalizing Cognitive Processing
The underlying philosophy guiding all work across Karunya institutional projects (including `course-website` and **Project Genesis**) stems from a fundamental design paradigm: **System-First Representation over Screen-First Design**.

Rather than treating educational software as a collection of disconnected CRUD pages or administrative forms, the architecture models **education itself as a unified computational system**:

1. **Cognitive Externalization**: Human working memory has finite limits. The architecture acts as a cognitive prosthesis, externalizing complex pedagogical structures, student memory retention curves (Ebbinghaus decay), and institutional knowledge DAGs into explicit, visual computational models.
2. **Systemic Representation**: Every module begins with the fundamental question: *"What system am I actually representing?"* rather than *"What UI screen should I build?"*
3. **The Triadic Architectural Hierarchy**:
   - **Layer 1 (Karunya Institutional Product Layer)**: The concrete academic experience and course delivery environment (`course-website`).
   - **Layer 2 (Genesis Computational Engine)**: The macro simulation engine modeling the physical, cognitive, economic, and social dynamics of educational systems over 50-year horizons.
   - **Layer 3 (SIH 26101 Specialized Domain Layer)**: Sector-specific workforce capacity building and closed-loop competency gap assessments for India's Official Statistical System (MoSPI DIID).

## 4. Daily Life Routines & Spatial Geographical Commute Modeling

- **Daily Schedule Cycle**: `Morning Commute` $\rightarrow$ `Instruction 1` $\rightarrow$ `Recess Break` $\rightarrow$ `Instruction 2` $\rightarrow$ `Lunch Break` $\rightarrow$ `Study Period` $\rightarrow$ `Evening Commute`.
- **Break Time Recovery**: Recess and lunch breaks replenish student stamina (`+12%`) and relieve daily stress (`-15%`).
- **Geographical Commute**: Each student has spatial coordinates $(x,y)$ on a regional map ($0.5\text{–}18\text{ km}$ radius), transport mode (walk, bike, bus, car), and travel time ($5\text{–}60\text{ mins}$).

## 5. The 8 Scientific Research Frameworks

Every simulation experiment automatically undergoes evaluation across:
1. **Five Questions Analysis**: WHAT, WHY, HOW, SHOULD THIS, WHY DO THIS.
2. **First Principles Deconstruction**: Irreducible assumptions about human cognitive load.
3. **Dependency Analysis**: Prerequisite DAG chains & structural bottlenecks.
4. **Context Survival Analysis**: Environmental limits where the system survives vs collapses.
5. **PESTLE Environment Analysis**: Political, Economic, Tech, Cultural, Educational, Demographic alignment.
6. **Multi-Stakeholder Incentive Analysis**: Game-theoretic incentives for students, teachers, parents, governments, and employers.
7. **Failure Propagation & Resilience**: Failure modes, stress cascades, and recovery speed.
8. **Emergence & 50-Year Trajectory**: Non-linear feedback loops and multi-generational outcomes.
